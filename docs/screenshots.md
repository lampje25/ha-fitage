# Screenshot plan

Use a dedicated demonstration Home Assistant instance with fictional data. Capture images at a consistent browser size, use a neutral theme, and crop them to the relevant interface. Review every image at full resolution before publishing it.

## Planned images

1. **Integration overview** — FITAGE visible under **Settings → Devices & services**, with only aggregate device and entity counts.
2. **Profile devices** — two fictional profiles, for example `Alex Example` and `Morgan Demo`, showing that profiles are separate devices.
3. **Body-composition sensors** — a small selection such as weight, BMI, body fat, body water, muscle, and heart rate, populated with explicitly fictional values.
4. **Weight graph** — a Recorder-backed weight statistic with generated values and dates.
5. **Statistics opt-in** — the integration options screen showing historical statistics disabled by default and a fictional selection after opt-in.
6. **Period view** — the standard statistics graph over a clearly labelled fictional 30-day period.

## Privacy checklist

- Use only fictional profile names and generated or clearly anonymized values.
- Show no email address, FITAGE user ID, measurement ID, device MAC address, token, credential, config-entry ID, authorization header, or diagnostic payload.
- Keep browser tabs, notifications, sidebars, URLs, and developer-tool panels free of personal information.
- Do not derive examples from a real person's health history, even after rounding values.
- Check image metadata and filenames before publishing.

Store approved images in a future `docs/images/` directory and add descriptive alt text when embedding them. No screenshots are included yet.
